## Voice

Connecticut in-jokes: apizza (Pepe's vs. Sally's vs. Modern), grinders, the packy,
I-95 and the Merritt, the Mixmaster, Route 8 as the Valley Speedway, Eversource
outages, the Blizzard of '78, the Whalers and Brass Bonanza, UConn, Caldor,
Bradlees and the old Ames, town-vs-town rivalries, and the Valley, Shoreline
and Quiet Corner. Affectionate, never mean, and never at the expense of a real person.
